ESAS-r Symptom Assessment

Record how symptoms feel now, on a scale from 0 (none or best) to 10 (worst possible). The nine core domains are reported separately; the total is descriptive.

Not an emergency or prescribing tool. Review individual symptom ratings with a qualified clinician. No validated triage threshold or treatment recommendation is inferred from the sum. This is an independent assessment interface, not a replacement for the official ESAS-r form.
